Title: Your AI Agent Should Not Have This Permission

## PB-PROC-S02 | The AI Vendor Exit Test
Hypothesis: an overlooked procurement risk attracts founder/COO searches and qualified comments.
Audience: procurement leaders, CFOs, founders.
Hook type: counterintuitive checklist.
Target duration: 48–58 seconds.
Title: Before Buying AI, Run This Exit Test
Thumbnail frame: "CAN YOU LEAVE?" with export arrow and one locked icon.
Narration:
"Before signing an AI vendor contract, test how your business would leave. Ask for an export of sample records. Verify the files open in a second system. Check whether prompts, logs, workflows, and agent configurations are portable. Then read the contract for termination assistance, transition fees, deletion timelines, and access to backups. A CSV export alone is not enough if your business logic stays locked inside the vendor. Run a small exit rehearsal before committing to a long contract. PrismBay AI helps executives evaluate practical AI business systems."
Visual sequence: contract > export > integrity check > rebuild workflow > decision card.
