The real problem

Quoted revenue does not equal usable cash.

Contractors often pay for materials and labor before the customer pays the remaining balance. A profitable-looking estimate still leaves a funding gap at the start of a job.

Protect margin before offering discounts

Separate actual direct costs, overhead, contingency, desired margin, estimated sales tax, discounts, and deposit amounts.

Check the initial funding shortfall

Estimate what portion of direct costs falls due upfront, whether the deposit covers the outlay, and what deposit percentage would cover the exposure.

Stress test material and labor increases

Review the effect of a higher direct-cost scenario while holding the customer quote fixed. Spot an unprofitable bid before approval.

Prepare a professional customer estimate

Print or save a customer-facing estimate as PDF. Internal costs, profit and business assumptions remain outside the printout.
