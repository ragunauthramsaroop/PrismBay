A five-step working checklist

  1. 01

    Clarify the complete scope

  2. 02

    Count direct and indirect delivery time

    Include research, revisions, communication, coordination and quality assurance.

  3. 03

    Estimate real expenses

    Include paid tools, suppliers, travel and other costs attributable to the work.

  4. 04

    Apply contingency and target margin

    Stress-test the estimate against extra delivery hours and realistic customer expectations.

  5. 05

    Confirm acceptance and change control

    Use a written approval process for scope changes, milestones and payment terms.

Frequently asked questions

Are gross margin and markup the same?

No. Gross margin measures gross profit divided by the selling price. Markup measures gross profit divided by cost.

Are transaction fees included in the free calculator?

No. Payment processing, taxes and other financing costs require separate review.
