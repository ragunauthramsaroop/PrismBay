A five-step working checklist

  1. 01

    Write the exact commitment

    Record what was agreed in plain language. Avoid broad summaries such as “support the community.”

  2. 02

    Assign one accountable owner

    A named role or person should know the next action. Note an escalation route when the primary owner is unavailable.

  3. 03

    Record the agreed date

    Separate dates promised externally from internal progress milestones.

  4. 04

    Attach closure evidence

    A completed activity needs a verifiable receipt, decision record or accepted output.

  5. 05

    Review unresolved items

    Prioritize overdue and high-impact actions before the next stakeholder meeting.

Frequently asked questions

Is a stakeholder register the same as a contact list?

No. A contact list stores identities. A commitments register also tracks what was promised, by whom, when and how closure was verified.

Should I put sensitive stakeholder data in this free CSV?

Use only information you are authorized to store. Keep personal, confidential and sensitive details in approved secure systems.
