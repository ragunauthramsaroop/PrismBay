A five-step working checklist

  1. 01

    State the decision on page one

    Describe the specific approval or direction sought and by whom.

  2. 02

    Group material claims

    Identify what is a verified fact, calculation, forecast or management assumption.

  3. 03

    Preserve the source date and scope

    Avoid citing information without its reporting period, context or applicable limitations.

  4. 04

    Surface counterarguments and risk

    Explain alternatives, downside conditions and implementation responsibilities.

  5. 05

    Review before circulation

    Confirm each claim against its source and record who cleared the final document.

Frequently asked questions

Is a board brief the same as a white paper?

No. A board brief supports a defined management decision. A white paper often presents a broader researched position, options or methodology.

Is AI-generated evidence acceptable without review?

No. All material facts and citations need verification against accessible primary sources.
