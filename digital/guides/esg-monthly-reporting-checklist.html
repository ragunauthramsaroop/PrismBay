A five-step working checklist

  1. 01

    Define the metric before collection

    Record the exact unit, period, scope and reporting boundary.

  2. 02

    Document the original source

    Keep a reference to the primary record and note changes from previous periods.

  3. 03

    Name the accountable data owner

    Distinguish the source owner from the person approving the published figure.

  4. 04

    Identify estimates and gaps

    Mark unresolved inputs and assumptions instead of treating blanks as zero.

  5. 05

    Complete review and sign-off

    Keep an audit trail of corrections, approvals and open actions before publication.

Frequently asked questions

Should estimated data be excluded?

Estimated figures need clear labeling, documented methods and review. Applicable reporting rules determine whether and how they are included.

Does a monthly ESG tracker certify compliance?

No. The worksheet organizes evidence and review responsibilities. It does not provide assurance, legal verification or certification.
