How to interpret your result

In the fictional starting example, 100 attempts produce 80 accepted outputs. The team spends $40 on tools and $60 on review. Apparent cost is $1 per attempt. Effective cost is $1.25 per accepted result. Acceptance is 80%, below a hypothetical 90% quality gate. Lower unit cost alone does not prove ROI.

Before approval, define the acceptance test, compare with the current workflow using the same quality standard, count setup and oversight costs, and verify measured savings with actual records. This calculator is an educational decision aid, not a financial forecast or vendor benchmark.
