Why these controls matter

Why block execution even after a good demo?

One successful test does not establish permission safety. Enforce access restrictions in the connected service, then test forbidden calls directly.

Why treat invoice instructions as untrusted?

Documents are task inputs, not system authorities. A request inside a supplier PDF to ignore instructions or change bank details must not override approved policy.

What is a critical failure?

Missing enforced least privilege, credential separation, untrusted-input handling, exact-action approval, single-action authorization or emergency shutdown creates a release hold in this worksheet.
