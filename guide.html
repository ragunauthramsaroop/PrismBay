Free AI Vendor Risk Matrix

PrismBay AI | A 15-minute, evidence-first worksheet

Before approving an AI vendor, compare evidence rather than marketing claims. Copy this worksheet into your own procurement review. All example vendors below are fictional; this is educational guidance, not legal, security or procurement advice.

How to use it

Define your use case, data involved, proposed location, budget and accountable decision owner. Ask every vendor the same questions, attach dated evidence, identify a reviewer and set a review date.

Score 0 = unknown, 1 = vendor assertion without reviewed evidence, 2 = supporting evidence reviewed by a named owner. The total helps visualize missing evidence but never approves a supplier automatically. An unresolved critical issue requires human review regardless of score.

Stop-and-review conditions

Hold a decision for specialist review when sensitive data lacks adequate contractual protection, mandatory human approval is absent, exit or deletion is untested, or an important security claim has no supporting evidence. Record the risk owner, requested evidence and next review date.

Fictional worked example

Vendor A quotes less but cannot explain retention, demonstrate an export or identify an incident owner. Vendor B provides dated terms, a tested sample export and a named contact. Neither is automatically approved: independently verify the claims and evaluate fit for your particular use case.

A practical 30-day plan

Week 1: Choose one narrow use case and appoint a decision owner. Week 2: Request the evidence and test a sample export using non-sensitive data. Week 3: Review gaps with privacy, security, procurement and business owners. Week 4: Defer, pilot within documented boundaries or reject; record the rationale and set a reassessment date.

Keep a decision record: Vendor | Use case | Evidence URL/version | Reviewer | Review date | Open risk | Decision | Decision date.
