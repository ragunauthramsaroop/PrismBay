Questions shoppers actually ask

Clear answers reduce hesitation.

Why does the button say “Check route & availability”?

Because PrismBay Clean validates the supplier route, stock and destination shipping before payment rather than showing an unverified “in stock” claim.

Are the displayed prices discounted?

The displayed prices are the current listed prices. We do not use a fake crossed-out price or claim a percentage discount unless the same discount can be honored at checkout.

Do you show live stock counts?

Not unless the supplier evidence is current enough to support the exact number. We prefer a real availability check over a persuasive but unreliable counter.

Will there be bundles?

Bundle offers can be introduced when supplier stock, shipping and contribution economics support them. Any genuine saving should appear clearly before payment.
