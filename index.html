Frequently asked questions

Clear answers before checkout.

Where does payment happen?

Product buttons open a Stripe-hosted page at buy.stripe.com. Payment card information is entered there, not into the PrismBay Clean storefront.

Where do you currently accept orders?

The current launch is limited to eligible U.S. delivery addresses. Shipping eligibility depends on the product and destination.

How do I get order support?

Email support@prismbayai.com with the email used at checkout and enough order detail for the support team to locate the purchase. Do not send full card numbers by email.

What if my item arrives damaged or incorrect?

Contact support promptly with the order details and a description of the issue. Refund or replacement eligibility is reviewed from the order and fulfillment record.

Do you show customer ratings?

Only evidence-backed customer feedback should appear on this storefront. No rating is shown today because the store does not publish invented reviews or sales counts.

What happens if fulfillment is unavailable?

The order is reviewed rather than silently substituted with an unverified product. Support handles any required refund or next step for the affected order.
