Good to Know

Where do you currently ship?

Launch orders are limited to eligible U.S. addresses. Destination shipping and availability are confirmed before payment is released.

Why does checkout sometimes require confirmation?

Because PrismBay Clean keeps stock, supplier-route and final shipping claims fail-closed until verified for the order.

Do you use fake reviews or scarcity?

No. Customer-facing pages do not use fabricated reviews, sales counts, scarcity or stock claims.

What if a supplier route fails?

Checkout remains blocked or a verified alternative route is used rather than silently substituting an unverified product.
