Frequently asked questions

Clear answers before checkout.

Where does payment happen?

Product buttons open a Stripe-hosted page at buy.stripe.com. Payment card information is entered there, not into the PrismBay Clean storefront.

Where do you currently accept orders?

The current launch is limited to eligible U.S. delivery addresses. Shipping eligibility depends on the product and destination.

Are these sale prices?

The prices shown are the current listed product prices on this storefront. Promotional savings are labeled only when the same offer is supported at checkout.

How do I get order support?

Email support@prismbayai.com using the email associated with checkout and enough order detail for support to locate the purchase. Do not send full card numbers by email.

What if my item arrives damaged or incorrect?

Contact support promptly with order details and a description of the issue. Refund or replacement eligibility is reviewed using the order and fulfillment record.

Do you publish ratings or sales counts?

Only evidence-backed customer feedback should appear here. The storefront does not publish invented ratings, fabricated sales counts or false scarcity.
