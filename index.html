Commerce integrity

Expansion without pretending every mapped item is ready.

PrismBay Clean separates catalog discovery from purchase authorization. This keeps the storefront growing while withholding checkout where fulfillment evidence is still incomplete.

1 · Product mappingThe product must have a stable PrismBay SKU and exact commercial identity.
2 · Supplier and stock evidenceSupplier variant identity and current inventory must be verified rather than inferred.
3 · Destination freight and economicsShipping to the buyer destination and unit economics must meet the current commerce thresholds.
4 · Listing rights and checkoutPublic media/listing rights and a verified checkout route must be present before purchase is exposed.
5 · Post-payment controlsPaid orders enter the controlled order, status and fulfillment workflow. Supplier-order spending is not implied by catalog visibility.

Straight answers

Catalog FAQs

The storefront now shows both live-checkout products and products still moving through commercial verification.

Why do some products not have a Buy button?

Those items are mapped into the PrismBay physical catalog but public checkout remains withheld while required supplier, stock, destination-freight, economics, listing-rights and checkout checks are completed.

Does “Checkout live” mean the item is always in stock?

No. “Checkout live” identifies the public Stripe purchase route. It is not a stock claim. Order processing and fulfillment remain subject to the store policies and current operating checks.

Where is my card information entered?

Products with a live purchase route open Stripe-hosted checkout. PrismBay Clean does not ask you to send card information by email or message.

Will more products appear?

Yes. The catalog architecture is now designed to expand. Additional products should move into public checkout only after their required commerce verification is complete.

How do I contact support?

Email support@prismbayai.com or use the Contact page linked throughout the site.
