How ordering works

A simple path from product to payment.

PrismBay Clean keeps product discovery, checkout and customer policies easy to understand.

1 · Choose a productReview the product details, use cases and displayed USD price.
2 · Open secure checkoutThe product button takes you directly to a Stripe-hosted checkout page.
3 · Enter delivery detailsProvide the information requested by Stripe for payment and order processing.
4 · Order processing beginsAfter successful payment, the order enters the PrismBay Clean processing workflow.
5 · Support stays availableUse the Contact page or support@prismbayai.com for order questions and assistance.

Shopping FAQs

Useful information before you place an order.

Where is my card information entered?

Product purchase buttons open Stripe-hosted checkout. PrismBay Clean does not ask you to send card information by email or message.

Where can I review shipping and returns information?

Shipping and Returns & Refunds pages are linked in the policy section and footer so you can review them before ordering.

Will more products be added?

Yes. New products are added to the public shop only after they have a complete customer-facing listing and approved public checkout route.

How do I contact support?

Email support@prismbayai.com or use the Contact page linked throughout the site.
