About PrismBay Clean

A focused online retail storefront built around a smaller product collection, visible pricing and direct hosted checkout.

Business identity

PrismBay Clean is a customer-facing retail brand operated by STUDYSMARTZ LLC.

Store model

The storefront focuses on practical home-cleaning products selected for clear use cases. The current launch collection is intentionally small so shoppers can compare products without an oversized catalog.

Payments

Product purchase links open Stripe-hosted checkout at buy.stripe.com. Payment card details are entered on Stripe's checkout service rather than on the PrismBay Clean storefront.

Fulfillment

Orders use third-party fulfillment and carrier routes. PrismBay Clean does not present fabricated inventory counts, delivery guarantees or customer ratings. If fulfillment is unavailable, the affected order is handled through support rather than silently substituted.

Customer support

Support is available at support@prismbayai.com. Shipping, returns, privacy and store terms are published on this domain.
