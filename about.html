About PrismBay Clean

A focused online retail storefront built around clear use cases, visible pricing, direct hosted checkout and customer policies designed to reduce guesswork.

Business identity

PrismBay Clean is a customer-facing retail brand operated by STUDYSMARTZ LLC.

Why the catalog is small

The current collection is intentionally focused. Each product addresses a distinct cleaning problem so shoppers can compare options without sorting through an oversized catalog or dozens of near-duplicate listings.

How products are presented

Product cards show the intended use, current listed U.S. dollar price and a direct path to the matching Stripe-hosted checkout. PrismBay Clean does not use fabricated ratings, fake sales counts, false stock counters or artificial countdown timers.

Checkout approach

Payment takes place on Stripe-hosted checkout at buy.stripe.com. Payment-card information is entered on Stripe's service rather than into the PrismBay Clean storefront.

Fulfillment approach

PrismBay Clean uses third-party suppliers and fulfillment routes. Stock and delivery timing are confirmed after payment. If a paid order cannot be fulfilled through a legitimate route, PrismBay Clean issues a full refund rather than silently substituting a materially different product.

Customer information first

Shipping, returns, privacy and store terms are published on clean.prismbayai.com so customers can review the operating model before ordering.

Customer support

Order and store support is available at support@prismbayai.com. Customers should never send full card numbers, card security codes, passwords or online-banking credentials by email.
