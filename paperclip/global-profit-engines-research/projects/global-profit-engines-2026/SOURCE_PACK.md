## Important forensic findings already supported by the primary sources

1. The infographic is not a synchronized TTM ranking. It mixes at least four fiscal windows: Microsoft Jun-2026, NVIDIA Jan-2026, Apple Sep-2025, and Dec-2025 for most remaining companies.
2. Aramco requires an ownership-basis footnote rather than a numerical correction. The audited 2025 statement reports $93.389B of total group net income and $92.811B attributable to shareholders. The graphic's $92.8B matches the shareholder-attributable figure after rounding.
3. Berkshire's approximately $67.0B headline net earnings include material investment gains/losses. The 2025 annual report states net earnings included approximately $30.7B of after-tax investment gains and $8.3B of after-tax impairment losses on equity-method investments. A profit-quality section must separate operating earnings from GAAP net earnings.
4. JPMorgan's approximately $57.0B is a bank result. Industrial operating-margin and conventional FCF comparisons are not appropriate. Use net interest income, noninterest revenue, credit costs, CET1, ROTCE, efficiency ratio, deposits and capital distributions.
5. TSMC's annual report reports 2025 net income of NT$1,717.88B and a company-disclosed US-dollar equivalent of $55.21B, with operating margin 50.8% and net margin 45.1%.
6. Microsoft FY2026 Form 10-K reports revenue of approximately $331.8B, operating income of approximately $155.2B and GAAP net income of $133.749B. The company also presents non-GAAP measures excluding specified OpenAI investment effects. Any OpenAI analysis must preserve GAAP/non-GAAP separation.
7. NVIDIA FY2026 Form 10-K reports revenue $215.938B, operating income $130.387B and net income $120.067B. Data Center revenue increased 68% year over year. This should anchor the semiconductor section.
8. Apple FY2025 Form 10-K reports revenue $416.161B, operating income $133.050B and net income $112.010B. Services revenue was $109.158B. The filing reports common-stock repurchases of $90.052B for the year.
9. Alphabet FY2025 Form 10-K reports revenue $402.836B, operating income $129.039B and net income $132.170B. Other income, net, was $29.787B, which means profit growth decomposition must isolate non-operating effects.
10. Amazon FY2025 Form 10-K reports net income $77.670B. Workers should extract consolidated and segment data directly from the filing before making AWS/retail comparisons.
11. Meta FY2025 Form 10-K reports revenue $200.966B, operating income $83.276B and net income $60.458B. Effective tax rate was 30% for 2025 versus 12% for 2024, a material driver of year-over-year net-income movement. Causal attribution beyond the disclosed provision movement remains a separate research question.
