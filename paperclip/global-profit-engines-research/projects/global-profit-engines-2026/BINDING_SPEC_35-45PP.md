# Global Profit Engines 2026 - Phase II binding publication specification

Status: binding build specification for PaperClip command #291 after Phase II council review.

Target: 38 dense pages, acceptable band 35-45 pages. No decorative blank pages. No padding. No false precision.

## Publication architecture

1. Cover + publication note.
2. Executive judgment.
3. Board findings + assurance legend.
4-5. Infographic reconstruction + fiscal-window mismatch.
6-7. Latest-fiscal-year baseline + synchronized-LTM methodology.
8-9. Synchronized-LTM panel with explicit quality warnings.
10-11. Earnings-quality framework: operating vs net, OCF/net, capex intensity, FCF treatment, depreciation lag.
12-26. Ten company dossiers, maximum 1.5 pages each, organized by economic lane rather than alphabetical isolation.
27-30. Cross-cutting AI capex, balance sheet, distributions, concentration, regulation and geopolitics.
31-33. Scenarios + durability matrix.
34-38. Board/investor implications, red-flag register, calculation audit trail, source register, forensic verdict.

A. Original graphic versus verified figure, period and basis.

Delete any standalone ordinal LTM league table, industrial margin/FCF ranking containing JPMorgan, TSMC reconstructed USD LTM without a fixed flow-FX method, decimal durability scoring, and quantified OpenAI/export-control claims not directly disclosed.

## Assurance taxonomy

Every material number must carry one status:
- AUDITED VERIFIED
- OFFICIAL UNAUDITED VERIFIED
- INDEPENDENTLY CALCULATED
- ESTIMATED
- NOT VERIFIABLE

Microsoft FY2026 equals the June-2026 anchor and is AUDITED VERIFIED rather than recomputed LTM. Recomputed LTM rows remain INDEPENDENTLY CALCULATED.

## Mandatory analytical controls

- The original graphic is a latest-reported fiscal-year snapshot, not one synchronized TTM ranking.
- Aramco $92.8B reconciles to $92.811B shareholder-attributable profit. Total group net income was $93.389B. Adjusted net income and FCF remain separate non-IFRS lanes.
- Alphabet 2025 other income of $29.787B and H1-2026 securities gains must be isolated from recurring operations.
- Berkshire GAAP net earnings must not be used as an operating proxy. Any rough operating-centered bridge remains INDEPENDENTLY CALCULATED until replaced by company-defined operating earnings.
- JPMorgan remains outside industrial margin and FCF rankings. Use bank metrics.
- TSMC uses NT$ as the primary analytical currency. Company-disclosed USD annual values may be quoted with the translation basis stated.
- Meta tax-provision movement may be described. Causal attribution beyond disclosure stays unresolved.
- OpenAI economics beyond Microsoft's public GAAP/non-GAAP disclosures stay out of the report.

## Page and typography system

A4, single-column body.
Margins: 20mm left/right, 17mm top, 16mm bottom.
Body: 9.5pt serif, 14-14.5pt leading, dark ink.
Part title: 16pt bold sans, compact opener, no full blank divider page.
Section title: 11-12pt bold sans.
Caption/source: 7.6-8.1pt sans.
Tables: 7.8-8.2pt, repeat header rows, pale header fill, restrained rules.

Density target: 380-440 lexical words on text-heavy pages. Charts/tables may reduce word count if they occupy decision-useful space. No page may exist solely to create spacing.

## Visual system

Institutional palette: navy, ink, muted blue, restrained gold accent, pale neutral fill. No gradients, shadows, 3D effects, decorative icons, or magazine-style clutter.

Only three callout types:
- AUDIT VERDICT
- ACCOUNTING TRAP
- BOARD IMPLICATION

Every exhibit footer states period, unit/currency, accounting basis, assurance status, reported versus calculated status, and source.

## Citation and reproducibility standard

Primary filings and official IR/regulatory sources control. Secondary sources may add context but never override core financial facts.

Every material table row must carry: company, metric, exact period, unit/currency, accounting basis, source, assurance status, and calculation note when derived.

LTM formula: latest audited annual item + current equal-length interim-period item - prior-year equal-length comparable interim-period item. Compute separately for every line item. Never average margins.

## Release gate

Release only when all are true:
1. Every headline figure traced to a primary source.
2. Mixed-period warning appears before any rank.
3. Recomputed LTM values are clearly labelled INDEPENDENTLY CALCULATED.
4. Microsoft anchor-coincident FY2026 is labelled AUDITED VERIFIED.
5. Sector-lane comparability rules are applied.
6. Non-GAAP/non-IFRS measures are identified.
7. Red-flag and unresolved-data registers are visible.
8. No blank or near-blank padding pages.
9. No content page has avoidable trailing whitespace exceeding about 40% of page height.
10. All chart/table text is legible and unclipped.
11. TOC, page references, bookmarks and hyperlinks resolve.
12. PDF text is searchable and fonts are embedded/subset.
13. DOCX and PDF are rendered and visually inspected page by page.
14. SHA-256 is recorded for the release PDF.
