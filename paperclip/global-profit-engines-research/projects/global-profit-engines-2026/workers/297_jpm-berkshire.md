### Why industrial metrics fail

A bank does not have an industrial COGS structure. Conventional operating margin and free cash flow are poor cross-sector metrics because deposits, loans, securities, market funding and regulatory capital form the operating balance sheet itself.

Use these bank-specific metrics instead:

- net income and EPS
- net interest income and noninterest revenue
- provision for credit losses and net charge-offs
- CET1 capital and CET1 ratio
- ROE and ROTCE
- efficiency or overhead ratio
- average and period-end deposits and loans
- capital distributions
- trading, investment-banking and payments mix

### 2025 bank-specific signals

2025 ROE was 17% and ROTCE 20%.

Fourth-quarter 2025 net revenue was $46.8B on a managed basis, with $25.1B of net interest income and $21.7B of noninterest revenue. Fourth-quarter credit costs were $4.7B, including $2.5B of net charge-offs and a $2.1B net reserve build. The release states the reserve build included a $2.2B reserve for the forward purchase commitment of the Apple credit card portfolio.

Average loans grew 9% year over year in the fourth quarter and average deposits grew 6% year over year. The Commercial & Investment Bank reported 2025 ROE of 18%, while Asset & Wealth Management reported 2025 ROE of 40%.

### Forensic interpretation

The $57.0B headline should remain in the all-company profit-scale table, but JPMorgan should have a separate bank panel. The durability questions are funding franchise strength, credit normalization, fee and market revenue mix, capital requirements, deposit pricing and distribution capacity under stress tests.

Do not compare JPMorgan's conventional FCF or operating margin against Microsoft, NVIDIA, Apple or TSMC.

## Berkshire Hathaway

### Headline accounting result

Berkshire Hathaway reported $67.0B of net earnings attributable to Berkshire shareholders in 2025. The annual report states this figure included approximately $30.7B of after-tax investment gains and $8.3B of after-tax impairment losses on equity-method investments.

Primary source:
https://berkshirehathaway.com/2025ar/2025ar.pdf

### Operating components

After-tax earnings by major category, 2023 / 2024 / 2025, in $B:

Insurance underwriting: 5.428 / 9.020 / 7.258.
Insurance investment income: 9.567 / 13.670 / 12.513.
BNSF: 5.087 / 5.031 / 5.476.
Berkshire Hathaway Energy: 2.331 / 3.730 / 3.979.
Manufacturing, service and retailing: 13.362 / 13.072 / 13.647.
Investment gains: 58.873 / 41.558 / 30.737.
Other-than-temporary impairment of Kraft Heinz and Occidental investments: 0 / 0 / (8.255).

Berkshire shareholders' equity was $717.4B at Dec. 31, 2025, up $68.1B from Dec. 31, 2024.

### Forensic interpretation

The $67.0B GAAP figure is valid for an accounting-profit ranking but is not a stable operating earnings proxy. Mark-to-market investment gains create large year-to-year volatility unrelated to the operating businesses.

A rough arithmetic adjustment of $67.0B - $30.7B + $8.3B yields about $44.6B. Label this only as INDEPENDENTLY CALCULATED rough-cut operating-centered earnings. Do not call it Berkshire's official operating earnings until the company's own reconciliation is cited.

The final paper should show two Berkshire columns: GAAP net earnings and company-defined operating earnings. Insurance underwriting, insurance investment income, BNSF, BHE, manufacturing/service/retailing and investment gains should remain separate.
