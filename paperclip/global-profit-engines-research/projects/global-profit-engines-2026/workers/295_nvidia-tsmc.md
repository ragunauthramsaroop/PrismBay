## Fabless versus foundry economics

NVIDIA monetizes architecture, accelerators, networking and software around a fabless model. TSMC monetizes wafer capacity, process leadership, yield and utilization while carrying the capital burden of fabs and equipment.

NVIDIA's cycle risk therefore appears through end-demand concentration, pricing, purchase commitments, inventory and export restrictions. TSMC's cycle risk appears through utilization, node migration, capex, depreciation and customer mix.

NVIDIA's FY2026 operating margin calculated from audited inputs is 60.4%. TSMC's reported 2025 operating margin is 50.8%. These figures should not be used as a simple efficiency ranking because TSMC carries manufacturing capital and NVIDIA does not.

## Stress channels

Downside channel 1: hyperscaler digestion reduces accelerator orders. NVIDIA sees revenue and gross-margin pressure first. TSMC sees lower advanced-node and packaging utilization with a lag.

Downside channel 2: export-control tightening reduces addressable demand or forces product redesign and mix changes.

Downside channel 3: competing accelerators and custom silicon compress NVIDIA pricing while TSMC retains foundry demand but sees mix shift.

Upside channel: sustained AI infrastructure demand keeps NVIDIA Data Center growth elevated and maintains TSMC advanced-node utilization, supporting high margins despite capex.
