## Cross-company forensic findings

Alphabet and Amazon show major non-operating contributions to 2025 net income, so operating-income growth and cash conversion should sit beside net-income growth.

Meta's 2025 after-tax result understates operating expansion because the effective tax burden rose sharply.

Amazon and Meta show the clearest cash-flow pressure from accelerating infrastructure spend. Alphabet's 2026 capex guide signals a further step-up beyond 2025.

Apple remains structurally less capital intensive than the hyperscalers. Its Services mix and buyback program matter more to profit durability than data-center capex.

Microsoft's GAAP/non-GAAP OpenAI treatment requires explicit reconciliation before any claim about underlying AI profitability.
