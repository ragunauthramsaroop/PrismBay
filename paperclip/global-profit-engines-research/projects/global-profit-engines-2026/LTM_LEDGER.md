## Forensic interpretation

The synchronized LTM view changes the story materially. Alphabet rises to the top of the nine-company USD panel, but the jump is heavily affected by large gains on debt and equity securities in H1 2026. NVIDIA also rises sharply as AI infrastructure demand expands. Amazon's LTM net income moves well above its FY2025 result, while Microsoft remains at its audited FY2026 figure.

This is precisely why a single headline ranking is insufficient. A synchronized period improves timing comparability but can worsen earnings-quality comparability when non-operating market gains dominate. The final report must show LTM net income beside operating-income, operating-cash-flow and sector-specific quality measures rather than treating the LTM order as a standalone league table.

Mandatory caption for any LTM exhibit: "LTM net-income order is not a quality or durability ranking. Recomputed LTM figures are INDEPENDENTLY CALCULATED. Microsoft equals its AUDITED VERIFIED FY2026 annual result. See operating-income, cash-conversion and sector-lane panels."
