Own synchronized comparison logic, calculation audit trails and sector-specific comparability rules.

Use audited annual values for assurance-first tables. Use independently calculated LTM/TTM values only when source periods and formulas are explicit. Recalculate margins, growth, concentration shares, cash conversion, capex intensity and scenario sensitivities from full-precision inputs. Treat banks, conglomerates, energy producers, semiconductor companies and platform businesses according to their own economics rather than forcing one margin framework across all entities.
