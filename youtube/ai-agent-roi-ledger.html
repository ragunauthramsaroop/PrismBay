Model: baseline = volume × manual minutes ÷ 60 × hourly cost. Exception cost = volume × (1 − completion rate) × manual minutes ÷ 60 × hourly cost. Oversight = volume × review seconds ÷ 3600 × hourly cost. Monthly economic benefit = baseline − exceptions − oversight − recurring technology and risk costs. Simple payback = implementation ÷ positive monthly benefit. Real deployments require ramp-up, controls, financing and finance validation.
