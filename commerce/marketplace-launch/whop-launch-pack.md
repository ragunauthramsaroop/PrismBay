## Alternative channels, ordered by practical launch friction

- **Whop**: first-party marketplace + affiliate selling on platform, no listing subscription reported. Primary pilot.
- **Gumroad**: digital file marketplace + affiliates, but **Discover eligibility requires ≥$100 in genuine sales** and separate risk review. Not immediate traffic from day one. Fees: 30% on Discover sales; verify direct sale fees and payout rules.
- **Payhip**: digital marketplace, but **requires ≥$10 in genuine sales and store review** before marketplace acceptance. Affiliates are reported but payout to affiliates is handled by seller, not automatically by Payhip.
- **Creative Market**: curated creative-design template buyer audience, shop application required; assess product-fit and license, particularly for business spreadsheets.
- **Etsy**: strong discovery/offsite ads, but up-front listing fee ($0.20 per listing), potential setup fee, processing and transaction fees. Not zero-spend; original designs only. Hold under the zero-new-spend requirement.
