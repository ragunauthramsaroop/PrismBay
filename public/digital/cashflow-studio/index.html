Straight answers

Questions before you buy?

We want you to know what you are purchasing. Email support@prismbayai.com for product or order questions.

Is this a subscription?

No. Cashflow Studio is a one-time digital workbook purchase priced at $5.99 USD before any applicable tax shown at checkout.

Which software do I need?

Microsoft Excel or another application that opens .xlsx files is required. Some applications recalculate workbook formulas differently, so Excel is recommended for best compatibility.

Will the workbook connect to my bank?

No. Enter your own transactions manually. The workbook does not connect to your bank, fetch financial data or send your entries to PrismBay.

What happens after payment?

Stripe redirects you to a confirmation page. Select Verify payment and download. Our delivery service checks the payment and exact item, then provides a short-lived link to the ZIP file.

Does this replace an accountant?

No. This is a business-planning tracker, not financial, accounting, legal or tax advice. It does not file taxes or automatically reconcile bank statements.

What if my download fails or the workbook is defective?

Contact support@prismbayai.com with the purchased product and your checkout receipt reference, never card numbers. We review delivery issues, significant product defects and applicable refund requests. See purchase information.
