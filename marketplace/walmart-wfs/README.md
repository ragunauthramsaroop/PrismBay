## What the video says

The user-supplied 80-second video recommends finding Walmart out-of-stock listings, buying name-brand closeout merchandise from NexDeal at wholesale prices, sending bulk inventory to Walmart Fulfillment Services (WFS), and earning the retail spread after Walmart handles delivery. The example appears to show a Ninja Crispi at about $74.99/unit with MOQ 72 and a Walmart listing near $134. Exact UPC, model/color, condition, lot, availability and competitive Buy Box were not demonstrated. Treat both prices as an illustration, not an executable offer.

**This is inventory-funded marketplace wholesale resale, not zero-capital dropshipping.** WFS receives seller-funded stock at its warehouse and charges fulfillment/storage fees. An out-of-stock search does not prove profitable demand.

## Eight evidence gates before any order

1. APPROVED WALMART ACCOUNT: actual approved seller account of the real applicant, payments and document review completed.
2. WFS ENABLED: seller is enrolled and receiving destination is confirmed for this SKU.
3. EXACT PRODUCT: UPC/GTIN, model, variant, color, pack quantity, warranty, condition, packaging and market listing all match.
4. RESALE RIGHTS: documents sufficient for legal resale and any required brand privileges. Avoid brand restrictions and counterfeit/diversion risks.
5. VERIFIED SUPPLIER: live stock, supplier legal identity, dated SKU-level commercial invoice, order terms and permitted resale are documented.
6. INBOUND READY: landed freight, warehouse receiving, prep, labeling, cartons, damage allowances and any import fees are quoted.
7. RETURNS READY: return/recall process, compliance obligations, reserve, insurance as needed and warranty obligations are defined.
8. CURRENT DEMAND: valid competitive offer price, seller count, genuine sales demand and inventory velocity verified; stockout alone is not sufficient.

NexDeal's own terms warn it does not provide manufacturer authorization letters. Freight/pickup is buyer-paid; orders are final; individual consumer drop-ship is not offered. Do NOT treat NexDeal as approved for any SKU absent written supporting evidence.

## Execution path

**Step A: Eligibility.** Document the legal applicant, EIN/tax path and Walmart approval. No inventory purchases.

**Step B: Candidate list.** Research >=20 exact GTINs from verified wholesalers, preferring low MOQ, small pack size, low return exposure and clear brand rights. Reject mismatched or restricted variants.

**Step C: Economics.** Use the accompanying spreadsheet/model to stress-test each item at the lowest realistic marketplace offer with official current WFS fees, inbound freight and anticipated returns. Reject below-margin or high-capital exposure.

**Step D: Controlled lot.** Only after separate budget approval and all documentary gates, trial a small MOQ lot through confirmed WFS inbound. Do not accept 72-unit orders as an automatic starting point.

**Step E: Proof.** Track actual receiving, public listings, units paid, refunds, payout dates, stock aging, fees, and realized contribution. Expand inventory only on documented sale and payout evidence.

**Fallback:** If the real applicant is ineligible or denied by Walmart, use PrismBay Clean's existing US research storefront or another independently approved marketplace as a separate test, without claiming its physical checkout is already approved or ready. Comply with the existing verified-supplier and checkout gates described in the repository README.
