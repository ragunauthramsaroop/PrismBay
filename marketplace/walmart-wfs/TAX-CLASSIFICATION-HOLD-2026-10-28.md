## Tax documentation distinction

IRS single-member LLC default: https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/single-member-limited-liability-companies
IRS W-9 instructions: https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/fw9.pdf

If the disregarded LLC's owner is a **foreign person for U.S. tax purposes**, the IRS W-9 instructions direct the **owner** to the appropriate Form W-8 rather than a W-9, even if the owner has a U.S. TIN. Confirm the correct document with a qualified U.S. cross-border tax advisor; the owner's physical location alone is not enough to establish tax residency.

If foreign-owned, Form 5472 and a pro forma Form 1120 may be required for applicable reportable transactions. See IRS: https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i5472 (penalty for failure to file can be $25,000). Do not claim this document has been filed.

IRS S corporation rules prohibit nonresident-alien shareholders: https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/s-corporations
A genuine C corporation election under Form 8832 is legally different and carries material tax and recordkeeping consequences. Do NOT file an election to secure Walmart access without written advice from an independent U.S. cross-border CPA or attorney, and do not assert Walmart will approve after any election.
