How to use the worksheet

Turn missing information into a decision record.

You do not need a complex scoring algorithm to start a responsible AI vendor review. Ask consistent questions, require dated supporting documents and keep a named owner for every outstanding risk.

01

Ask the same questions

Examine retention, model-training terms, data export, security, human approval, ongoing monitoring and total cost.

02

Check actual evidence

Record a source document, test or contract clause. Mark unanswered assertions as unresolved rather than assuming compliance.

03

Assign an owner

Document who reviews the missing evidence, when to revisit the risk and who can approve or decline the use case.

Fictional worked example

Vendor A offers a lower quote. Vendor B supplies more evidence.

Vendor A has not documented data retention, demonstrated export or identified an incident owner. Vendor B has dated contractual terms and a sample export test. Neither is automatically approved. Review both against the intended use case, data sensitivity, evidence quality and risk appetite.
