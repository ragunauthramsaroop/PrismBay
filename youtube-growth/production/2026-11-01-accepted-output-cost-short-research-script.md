Here is a three-step procurement test.

First, divide model, integration, retries, and human review costs by correctly accepted outcomes.

Second, set a maximum cost per accepted outcome before the pilot.

Third, route simple work to cheaper models, and pause when retry costs exceed your limit.

A successful demo is not a business case. Compare your measured cost with the current workflow. Scale only after quality and controls pass.

Follow PrismBay AI for practical, measurable AI business systems."
