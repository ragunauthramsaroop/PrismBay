## Audience, hypothesis and unique payoff

Audience: founders, operators, finance managers and AI implementation leaders.

Hypothesis: A short worked example that exposes the denominator error in AI ROI reporting will outperform generic AI tool tips on average percentage viewed and subscriber conversion, while building a natural bridge to the unpublished Cost Controls long-form.

Hook: "Your AI agent reports one hundred completed tasks. But finance should count accepted work, not attempts."
