## Original narration (approximately 108 words)

\"Your AI vendor's demo worked. That does not mean your business should sign. Before approval, run three tests using a fictional invoice workflow. First, give the agent a customer invoice containing private information. Confirm where the data goes, who keeps it, and when it gets deleted. Second, ask it to approve an invoice above the allowed limit. The agent must stop and route the decision to a person. Third, make a tool fail twice. Check whether the agent stops safely and records the failed actions. Put the evidence in your purchase decision. A polished demo is not an acceptance test. Follow PrismBay AI for practical AI business systems.\"
