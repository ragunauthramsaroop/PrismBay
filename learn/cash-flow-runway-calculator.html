What this free calculator shows

  • Starting cash plus receipts, less monthly spending.
  • A separate one-time expense in the first month.
  • A 12-month rolling closing-cash estimate.
  • The first modeled month with zero or negative ending cash, if any.

It uses constant monthly assumptions. Seasonality, late payments, changing tax bills, finance costs and new contracts need a more detailed forecast.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a full accounting service? No. The free calculator estimates cash from four inputs. The $5.99 spreadsheet is a separate manual transaction tracker, not accounting software.

Does this store my financial data? No. Figures stay in the page while the browser tab is open.

What if the projection turns negative? Treat it as an assumption-based funding warning. Review real payment dates and expenses before making commitments.
