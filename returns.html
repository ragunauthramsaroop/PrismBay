Customer policy

Returns and Refunds

What to do when an order arrives damaged, defective, incorrect or materially different from its description.

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Report an order issue

Email support@prismbayai.com promptly with the email used at checkout, the product involved and a clear description of the issue. Photos or other supporting information may be requested when relevant.

Eligible issues

Return or refund review applies to concerns such as damaged, defective, incorrect or materially misdescribed items. Eligibility is determined from the order, fulfillment record, item condition and applicable consumer law.

Return instructions

Do not send an item to a supplier or other address without receiving return instructions from PrismBay Clean support. The correct return route depends on the order's fulfillment record.

Refund handling

Approved refunds are issued through the appropriate payment process for the order. Processing time after approval depends on the payment provider and financial institution.

Unavailable fulfillment

If a paid order cannot be fulfilled through a legitimate product route, PrismBay Clean handles the affected order rather than silently substituting an unverified product.

Payment information

Do not email full card numbers, security codes or online banking credentials. Stripe handles payment card entry on its hosted checkout pages.
