Shipping Policy

How PrismBay Clean handles delivery information for current U.S. orders.

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Current delivery market

PrismBay Clean currently accepts orders for eligible delivery addresses in the United States. Product availability and delivery support depend on the item, supplier route and destination.

Shipping details at checkout

Product buttons open Stripe-hosted checkout. The shipping information required for the order is entered during checkout. Any delivery information shown during checkout forms part of the order record.

Delivery timing

Delivery timing varies by product, supplier, carrier and destination. PrismBay Clean does not publish a guaranteed delivery date on the storefront unless the same commitment is supported by the order's fulfillment record.

Fulfillment review

After payment, the order enters PrismBay Clean order processing. If a legitimate fulfillment route is unavailable, PrismBay Clean will handle the affected order rather than silently substituting an unverified product.

Tracking and delivery issues

Where tracking is available, the relevant tracking information is supplied through the order or fulfillment process. For a delivery issue, email support@prismbayai.com using the email associated with the order.

Customer support

Do not send full payment card numbers by email. For order support, provide enough information for the support team to locate the purchase.
