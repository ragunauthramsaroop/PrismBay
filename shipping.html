Shipping Policy

What customers should know about delivery eligibility, order details, fulfillment review and delivery support before placing a PrismBay Clean order.

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Current delivery market

PrismBay Clean currently reviews physical-product shipping inquiries for eligible United States addresses. Physical-product checkout remains unavailable until the exact supplier variant, current stock, buyer-destination freight, full landed cost, media rights and fulfillment route pass a fresh commercial review. Approved physical orders, if enabled, are limited to eligible U.S. destinations.

Product price and checkout

Physical-product prices shown on the storefront are preliminary and exclude unverified destination freight. Product inquiry buttons do not charge customers or open a physical-product checkout. Following documented supplier, destination freight, landed-cost and fulfillment approval, an authorized Stripe-hosted checkout would display the final payable amount for review before payment. Separately, existing verified digital toolkits use their own Stripe-hosted checkouts and require no shipping.

Shipping address accuracy

Customers are responsible for entering a complete and accurate delivery address during checkout. Address errors may delay or prevent fulfillment. Contact support promptly if an address correction is needed after payment.

Stock and fulfillment confirmation

PrismBay Clean uses third-party suppliers and fulfillment routes. The exact supplier product and variant, stock, final buyer-destination freight and fulfillment eligibility must be confirmed before any physical-product payment is offered. Following a verified payment, the fulfillment team rechecks supplier availability and delivery feasibility before dispatch. If a legitimate route is unavailable, PrismBay Clean refunds the paid order rather than substituting an unverified product.

Delivery timing

Timing varies by product, supplier, carrier and destination. PrismBay Clean does not publish a guaranteed delivery date unless the same commitment is supported by the order's fulfillment record.

Tracking

Where tracking is available, tracking information is supplied through the relevant order or fulfillment process. Carrier scan timing and delivery-event updates remain subject to the carrier's systems.

Delivery problems

For a missing, delayed or delivery-related issue, email support@prismbayai.com from the email associated with checkout and provide enough order detail for support to locate the purchase.

Payment security

Do not send full card numbers, security codes, passwords or online-banking credentials by email. Card entry takes place on Stripe's hosted checkout service.
