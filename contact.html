Use the support route below for order, delivery, return, refund, product or privacy questions.

Protect your payment details

PrismBay Clean product payments take place on Stripe-hosted checkout. Never email full card numbers, card security codes, passwords or online-banking credentials.

Review how ordering works →

For order support

Include the email used at checkout, the product name and enough order information to identify the purchase. If the issue involves delivery or item condition, describe what happened clearly.

For damaged, defective or incorrect items

Describe the issue and keep relevant packaging or evidence until support confirms the next step. Photos may be requested where useful for the review.

For unfulfillable orders

If supplier stock or a legitimate fulfillment route is unavailable after payment, PrismBay Clean issues a full refund rather than silently substituting an unverified product.

For privacy questions

Clearly identify the privacy request and the email address associated with the relevant order or support interaction where applicable.
