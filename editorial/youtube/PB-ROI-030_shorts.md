## S1 | Two ledgers, one AI pilot | 45–55 seconds
Experiment: PB-ROI-030-S1
Audience: founders, CFOs, operations leaders.
Hypothesis: a counterintuitive finance opening and a visual split ledger outperform a generic productivity claim.
Working title: "AI Saved 67 Hours. The Cash Saving Was Only $90."
Alternative: "Your AI ROI Spreadsheet Has Two Different Ledgers"
Hook style: contrast, direct numbers. CTA: subscribe for practical AI business systems.
Original visual identity: warm paper background, black typography, blue left ledger for capacity, amber right ledger for cash. Hand-drawn receipt arrows. Avoid the channel's standard dark gradient template.

VOICEOVER (approximately 113 words):
"Your AI pilot saved sixty-seven staff hours. So did your company save two thousand three hundred dollars? Not necessarily. Here's a hypothetical example. One thousand supplier requests took twelve minutes each. With AI-assisted drafting, they take eight. That's sixty-six point seven hours of capacity released. At thirty-five dollars per hour, the capacity has an illustrative value of about two thousand three hundred thirty-three dollars. But unless spending disappears, that's not cash saved. Suppose thirty overtime hours are genuinely eliminated. Cash avoided: one thousand fifty dollars. Now subtract nine hundred sixty dollars of recurring operating costs. Net cash benefit: ninety dollars per month. Two ledgers. Different decisions. PrismBay AI explains practical AI business systems."

## S2 | The missing cost stack | 45–55 seconds
Experiment: PB-ROI-030-S2
Audience: procurement managers, finance controllers, AI implementation owners.
Hypothesis: showing overlooked cost categories using a growing invoice improves qualified watch time.
Working title: "The $420 AI Tool That Costs $960 a Month"
Alternative: "Your AI Pilot Budget Is Missing These 3 Costs"
Hook style: invoice reveal. CTA: comment with the cost category missing from your pilot budget.
Original visual identity: off-white invoice sheet, red correction marks, mint highlights, 2D receipt tear animation. No split ledger.

VOICEOVER (approximately 112 words):
"Your AI tool costs four hundred twenty dollars a month. Why does the pilot cost nine hundred sixty? Because the subscription is only one line. Consider this hypothetical workflow. Software and model usage: four hundred twenty dollars. Integration and maintenance: three hundred. Additional quality review: two hundred forty. Total recurring operating cost: nine hundred sixty dollars. Now suppose verified overtime savings equal one thousand fifty dollars monthly. Your net cash benefit is ninety dollars, before recovering the initial setup expense. If setup costs fifteen hundred dollars, simple payback takes about sixteen point seven months. None of these figures describes a real company. The lesson: price the entire operating system, not the tool. Follow PrismBay AI for practical AI investment decisions."

SHOT MAP:
0–3s: receipt "AI TOOL $420" expands into "ACTUAL RUN RATE $960".
3–16s: receipt line items animate one at a time with distinct icons.
16–23s: total $960 lands, one beat pause.
23–34s: cash avoided $1,050 less $960 equals $90.
34–45s: $1,500 setup / $90 net = 16.7 months.
45–55s: one-screen checklist: Usage, Integration, Review, Setup.
Caption QC: clearly distinguish recurring from one-time expenses; no "subscription $960" wording.
Experiment success: 1.5x comparable median watch minutes at 72h, above-median saves/shares if exposed.

## S3 | ROI passed, controls failed | 45–55 seconds
Experiment: PB-ROI-030-S3
Audience: executive sponsors, risk officers, procurement approvers.
Hypothesis: a human-approval control dilemma retains viewers beyond a pure ROI calculation.
Working title: "Your AI Pilot Makes Money. Would You Still Stop It?"
Alternative: "Three Gates Before You Scale an AI Agent"
Hook style: decision dilemma. CTA: subscribe for business-ready AI governance.
Original visual identity: technical process map on neutral gray grid, one coral STOP branch, one green HUMAN APPROVAL branch. No invoice, no ledgers.

VOICEOVER (approximately 107 words):
"Suppose an AI pilot shows positive financial returns. Would you approve expansion? Not until three gates pass. First, value. Did the pilot create verified cash savings or measurable operating capacity after full costs? Second, quality. Did errors, rework and service delays stay within the agreed limits? Third, risk. Did the system respect data permissions, record decisions and preserve human approval? Consider a hypothetical supplier workflow. The AI drafts a recommendation, but it must never change banking details or release a payment. A positive spreadsheet cannot override a failed control. Expand when all three gates pass. Modify when evidence is incomplete. Stop when critical risk limits fail. PrismBay AI: practical AI business systems."

SHOT MAP:
0–3s: large "PROFITABLE" approval stamp, abruptly blocked by "NOT APPROVED".
3–14s: Gate 1: VALUE with verified evidence box.
14–25s: Gate 2: QUALITY with errors/rework checklist.
25–39s: Gate 3: RISK, diagram AI DRAFT -> HUMAN APPROVAL -> PAYMENT, locked forbidden route.
39–50s: EXPAND / MODIFY / STOP decision tree.
50–55s: channel promise, no generic logo animation.
Caption QC: "Hypothetical supplier workflow", no claims of actual incident, no autonomous payment demo.
Experiment success: 1.5x comparable median watch minutes at 72h plus positive subscriber conversion.
