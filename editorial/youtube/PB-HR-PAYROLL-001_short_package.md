## Final spoken script (approx. 116 words)
"Your AI agent finds a payroll mistake. Should it change someone's salary? No. Not on its own.

Picture a payroll sheet. One employee's overtime suddenly doubles. The agent spots the anomaly and checks the approved timesheet. That's detection.

Next, it drafts a correction with the original amount, the proposed amount, and the supporting record. That's a proposal, not a payment.

An authorized payroll reviewer verifies the evidence and approves the exact change. A separate system checks the approval before any update runs.

If the source data changes, the approval expires. If the check fails, nothing gets paid.

The lesson? Give AI permission to identify and prepare. Give accountable people authority over consequential changes.

PrismBay AI helps leaders build practical, controlled AI business systems."
