## Source and arithmetic audit

All numeric cases are hypothetical teaching examples, not measured customer outcomes:
- 1,000 × (12 − 8) ÷ 60 = **66.6667 hours**, rounded to **66.7**.
- 30 overtime hours × $35/hour = **$1,050 verified cash avoidance IF the expense disappears**.
- $420 software + $300 maintenance + $240 review = **$960 recurring cost**.
- $1,050 − $960 = **$90 recurring monthly net cash effect, before setup**.
- $1,500 setup ÷ $90/month = **16.67 months simple payback**, assuming the same recurring effect. Exclude from Shorts unless explained.
- $2,333 is an **imputed capacity value** at $35/hour, not a cash saving. Do not add to the $1,050 avoided overtime, as the underlying hours overlap.
