## Shorts: three differentiated derivatives, each independent
### PB-ROI-030-S1 | Capacity is not cash
Target: CFOs and finance owners. Hypothesis: numerical contradiction drives stop-scroll and qualified profile visits.
Duration target: 35-45 seconds.
Hook (first 1s): "67 hours saved. Zero dollars saved?"
Voiceover: "A team handles one thousand supplier requests a month. Each request takes twelve minutes. An AI assistant cuts the average to eight. That releases sixty-six point seven hours. At thirty-five dollars per hour, the capacity is worth about twenty-three hundred dollars. But if payroll, overtime and supplier costs stay unchanged, cash savings are zero. The pilot may still be useful. Measure capacity in one ledger and actual expense avoided in another. Never add them together. PrismBay AI turns AI claims into operating decisions."
Visual design: full-screen two-column ledger, custom animated minute counters, zero-dollar cash receipt. Different from existing static template. On-screen "HYPOTHETICAL".
Title: "AI Saved 67 Hours. Did It Save Any Money?"
Success proxy: viewed-vs-swiped-away, first-3s retention, AVD, subscriber conversion.

### PB-ROI-030-S2 | Break-even math
Target: founders evaluating software subscriptions. Hypothesis: counterintuitive small net benefit earns saves/shares.
Duration target: 35-45 seconds.
Hook (first 1s): "$1,050 saved. Only $90 gained."
Voiceover: "Here is the math behind an AI pilot. Suppose a team eliminates thirty overtime hours at thirty-five dollars per hour. That is one thousand fifty dollars in verified expense avoided. Software costs four hundred twenty. Maintenance costs three hundred. Quality review costs two hundred forty. Total monthly operating cost: nine hundred sixty. Net cash benefit: ninety dollars. With fifteen hundred dollars in setup, simple payback is about seventeen months. Ask for full operating costs before approving the rollout. All numbers are hypothetical."
Visual design: original animated waterfall chart, expense cards entering separately, final $90 balance. No recycled ledger scene.
Title: "Your AI Pilot Saved $1,050. The Net Gain Was $90."
Success proxy: completion, rewatches, finance-related comments.

### PB-ROI-030-S3 | Permission boundary
Target: procurement/operations leaders. Hypothesis: clear actionable safety boundary attracts decision-makers beyond generic AI-news viewers.
Duration target: 40-50 seconds.
Hook (first 1s): "Would you let an AI approve a supplier payment?"
Voiceover: "Use AI to read an approved request, draft a recommendation, and flag missing evidence. Do not give the assistant unrestricted authority to approve payments or change supplier banking details. Put human sign-off at the decision point. Log the recommendation, evidence and approver. Track exceptions and set a stop rule for serious failures. Your pilot needs three gates: financial value, service quality, and risk control. If one fails, do not scale. PrismBay AI explains practical AI systems for business."
Visual design: original animated workflow map: request -> AI draft -> evidence -> HUMAN APPROVAL -> payment; red blocked branch for bank-detail changes. Different style and information from S1/S2.
Title: "The AI Payment Rule Every Business Needs"
Success proxy: retention, qualified comments, subscribers gained.
