## Short PB-FSB-ROI-004: The AI savings claim that fails an audit
Audience: CFOs, founders, operations leaders.
Distinct experiment: animated spreadsheet teardown with an original numerical example.
Duration target: 52-60 seconds. Hook category: math reversal.
Proposed title: 'Your AI Saved 40 Hours. Did It Save Any Money?'
Opening on-screen text: '40 HOURS SAVED ≠ $ SAVED'
Narration script:
'Your AI pilot saves three minutes per task across eight hundred monthly requests. That is forty hours of released capacity. But forty hours is not automatically forty hours of payroll savings. Here is a hypothetical example. You verify five hundred dollars in reduced overtime, but spend four hundred dollars on the service and oversight. Net recurring cash benefit: one hundred dollars, before setup costs. Now check the error rate, approval exceptions, and turnaround time. If mistakes increased, the pilot may still fail. Track capacity, verified cash effects, service quality and control exceptions separately. PrismBay AI helps leaders test business systems against evidence, not impressive dashboards.'
