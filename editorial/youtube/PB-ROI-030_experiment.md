# PB-ROI-030 | Growth experiment and production brief
Updated: 2026-10-18. Channel: UCw2hs85TzIpdKwJtG-BSxzQ (@PrismBayAI). Status: SCRIPT READY, NOT PUBLISHED. Do not publish until human-readable audio, on-screen arithmetic and original visuals pass QC.

## Audience and hypothesis
Audience: CFOs, founders, operations directors and AI program owners evaluating a 30-day AI pilot.
Hypothesis: A worked ROI calculation that distinguishes labor capacity from verified cash savings will attract more qualified viewers and subscribers than a generic AI productivity roundup.
Unique payoff: The viewer learns a seven-field decision scorecard and three-gate approval rule.

## Long-form experiment
Format: 8–11 minute original narrated explainer, with original calculations, ledger graphics and a fictional supplier-request workflow.
Opening 0–2 seconds: "Your AI pilot saved 67 hours. Did it save a single dollar?"
First 20 seconds: Show 67 hours capacity, $1,050 documented overtime avoided, $960 ongoing costs, $90 net monthly benefit, and explain why the numbers are not interchangeable.
Narrative: workflow scope -> baseline -> capacity ledger -> cash ledger -> full costs -> downside/upside -> quality and risk gates -> one-page scorecard.
CTA after demonstrated value: "PrismBay AI helps founders, operators and executives build practical AI business systems. Subscribe for measurable workflows."

## Measurement
Capture first 6h, 24h, 72h and 7d where available: impressions, CTR, views, watch minutes, average view duration, retention drop-off, shares, comments, subscriber gain and traffic sources. Compare each metric against channel's median of the last five comparable uploads, not a universal viral benchmark. For Shorts, compare viewed-vs-swiped and average percentage viewed if available.
Decision: SCALE when >=1.5x median watch minutes and positive subscriber conversion with adequate sample; RETEST when topic engages but opening retention is weak; MODIFY when packaging underperforms with healthy viewer retention; STOP when both retention and subscriber conversion remain below median after 7 days. Small samples = INSUFFICIENT DATA, not STOP.
