## D. Two distinct benefit ledgers
Capacity released (hours) = (baseline total handling minutes - pilot total handling minutes) / 60, adjusted for volume and case mix when needed.
Potential labor-capacity value = released hours × documented fully loaded labor rate. This is not automatically a cash saving.
Verified avoided expense ($) = overtime, contractor invoices, or other expenses demonstrably reduced because of the pilot, net of offsetting changes.
Incremental contribution margin ($) = incremental revenue attributable to the pilot minus directly related variable costs, where evidence exists.
Avoid counting the same benefit in more than one line.

Monthly net cash benefit = verified expense avoided + attributable incremental contribution margin - monthly recurring costs.
Simple payback in months = one-time costs / monthly net cash benefit, only when net cash benefit is positive. This excludes discounting and changes in cash flow over time.
If monthly net benefit is zero or negative, simple payback is not achieved under those assumptions.

## G. Three mandatory decision gates
FINANCE: Measured net cash economics or an explicitly funded, documented capacity outcome meets the pre-agreed objective. PASS / MODIFY / FAIL
QUALITY: Error rate, rework, customer impact, and turnaround meet pre-agreed thresholds. PASS / MODIFY / FAIL
RISK: Data handling, access boundaries, approval controls, and incident response meet pre-agreed thresholds. PASS / MODIFY / FAIL

## H. Worked illustration, hypothetical only
1,000 requests per month. Baseline 12 minutes per request. Pilot 8 minutes per request.
Baseline hours = 1,000 × 12 / 60 = 200.0.
Pilot hours = 1,000 × 8 / 60 = 133.3.
Capacity released = 66.7 hours per month.
Potential capacity value at $35/hour = approximately $2,333. This is not booked as cash saved.
Verified overtime avoided = 30 hours × $35 = $1,050.
Monthly recurring costs = $420 software + $300 maintenance + $240 review = $960.
Monthly net cash benefit = $1,050 - $960 = $90.
One-time setup = $1,500. Simple payback = $1,500 / $90 = approximately 16.7 months.
Downside: 15 overtime hours avoided gives $525 - $960 = -$435 monthly.
Upside: 60 overtime hours avoided gives $2,100 - $960 = $1,140 monthly.
All numbers are invented for teaching. No results are claimed for any real organization.
