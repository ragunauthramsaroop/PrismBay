## Voiceover script (approximately 54–60 seconds)
"Weeks to minutes. Reuters reports that AI is helping Deutsche Bank speed up decisions for some smaller business loans.

Impressive. But faster decisions are not the same as proven financial returns.

If you're evaluating AI in your company, track three numbers.

One: time to decision, from a complete request to an authorized outcome.

Two: verified cash effect, after software, review, and correction costs.

Three: exception rate. How often must someone step in or fix the result?

Take a fictional example: two hundred cases per month, twenty minutes saved on each case. That's about sixty-seven hours of capacity.

Unless costs fall, revenue grows, or service improves in a measurable way, those hours are not automatically cash savings.

Measure the business outcome, not only the demo.

PrismBay AI helps founders and operators build practical AI business systems."
