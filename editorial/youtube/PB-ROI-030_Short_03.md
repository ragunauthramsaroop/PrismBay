## Narration
Your AI pilot looks profitable. Should you expand? First, pass three gates.

Gate one: finance. Did expenses fall, or did employees gain capacity? Track those outcomes separately.

Gate two: quality. Did errors, turnaround and rework improve? Speed without accuracy is not success.

Gate three: governance. Who approves sensitive decisions? Who reviews exceptions? Who pauses the workflow when an issue appears?

If all three pass, expand with clear limits. If evidence is weak, revise and retest. If serious risks remain unresolved, do not expand.

AI ROI is a measured decision, not a sales demo.
