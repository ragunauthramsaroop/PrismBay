## Derivative Short 1 | capacity versus cash
Experiment ID: PB-ROI-030-S1. Format: 40–50s, 1080×1920. Audience: CFOs and operations managers. Hook type: contradiction. Title: **67 Hours Saved. Only $90 in Net Cash Benefit?** Thumbnail/frame: “67 HOURS ≠ $2,333 CASH”. CTA: “Follow PrismBay AI for measurable AI systems.”

0–2s: “Your AI pilot saved sixty-seven hours. Your cash benefit might still be ninety dollars.”
2–9s: “A hypothetical team processes one thousand requests. Twelve minutes each becomes eight.”
9–17s: “That releases sixty-six point seven staff hours. At thirty-five dollars per hour, the capacity value is roughly twenty-three hundred dollars.”
17–29s: “But time released is not a payroll saving. If only thirty overtime hours disappear, verified cash avoided is one thousand fifty dollars.”
29–39s: “Subtract nine hundred sixty dollars in monthly operating costs. Net cash benefit: ninety dollars.”
39–47s: “Track capacity and cash in separate ledgers. PrismBay AI covers practical AI systems with measurable results.”

On-screen labels: HYPOTHETICAL EXAMPLE; 1,000 × (12−8)/60 = 66.7h; 30 × $35 = $1,050; $1,050−$960 = $90. Do not label capacity value as cash saved.
Hypothesis: counterintuitive math produces strong completion and shares. Threshold: 7-day engaged views and subscribers per 1,000 views above the median of the five preceding distinct Shorts; absent sufficient baseline, RETEST. Schedule at least 4 hours away from another Short.

## Derivative Short 2 | stop rule
Experiment ID: PB-ROI-030-S2. Format: 40–50s, 1080×1920. Audience: founders and AI procurement leads. Hook type: decision rule. Title: **When Should You Stop an AI Pilot? Three Gates.** Thumbnail/frame: “3 GATES TO SCALE”. CTA: “Subscribe for practical AI investment decisions.”

0–2s: “A faster AI workflow can still be a failed pilot.”
2–11s: “Gate one: economics. Record verified cash saved, separately from staff capacity released. Include integration, monitoring, and correction costs.”
11–22s: “Gate two: quality. Compare the error rate, turnaround, and exception handling against your pre-pilot baseline.”
22–33s: “Gate three: risk. Check access boundaries, human approvals, and incidents. A failed control blocks expansion even when the math looks attractive.”
33–45s: “Scale when the three gates pass. Modify when value is uncertain. Stop when unacceptable risk persists. PrismBay AI helps teams build accountable AI systems.”

On-screen: FINANCE / QUALITY / RISK, each independently evaluated. Do not imply a real-world audit or certification.
Hypothesis: a practical decision rubric produces higher saves/shares than a numerical surprise. Compare sequentially, not as a simultaneous Shorts A/B test. Success gate: shares per 1,000 views and subscriber conversion above the preceding five distinct Shorts, subject to sufficient data.

## Derivative Short 3 | downside scenario
