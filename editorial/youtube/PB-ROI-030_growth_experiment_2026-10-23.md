## Measurement and decision rules
Check 6h, 24h, 72h and 7d if data exists. Record exact public video ID, publish timestamp, duration, impressions, CTR (long-form), engaged views (Shorts), average view duration, audience retention, watch minutes, subscriber gain/loss, shares, and comments.
Baseline: derive median of last 10 comparable public videos when video-level analytics become available. Until then mark BASELINE UNAVAILABLE, not zero.
SCALE: ≥1.5× comparable median in 72h qualified watch minutes or engaged views, with subscriber conversion no worse than baseline and acceptable retention.
RETEST: ≥1.0× comparable median with strong viewer questions or a specific packaging weakness.
MODIFY: <1.0× baseline with clear, remediable hook, pacing or audience mismatch.
STOP: <0.5× baseline across two distinct attempts or policy/accuracy failure.
No classification without enough observations. No invented success metrics.
