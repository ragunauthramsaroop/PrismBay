# PB-CTRL-017-S1 | Does Your AI Agent Actually Stop?

HOOK [0-3s]
"Your AI agent has a stop button. But does pressing it remove its access?"

EXPLAIN [3-20s]
"Stopping the chat is not the same as cancelling queued actions or revoking the agent's credentials. A workflow may still have permissions in connected tools."

PRACTICAL TEST [20-43s]
"Before production, test three things in a sandbox. First, stop the agent during a queued task. Second, verify no further action executes. Third, confirm the connected systems reject its previous access. Record the result and the owner responsible."

PAYOFF AND CTA [43-55s]
"If any step fails, keep the agent in draft-only mode. PrismBay AI helps business leaders deploy practical AI systems with measurable controls."
