## Reproducible fictional arithmetic
1000 requests per option. No real company data.
A: 1000 * $0.10 inference = $100. Review: 1000 * $0.20 = $200. Rework: 300 * $0.30 = $90. Total $390. Accepted outputs 700. Cost per accepted output = $390 / 700 = $0.55714, rounded $0.56.
B: 1000 * $0.25 inference = $250. Review: 1000 * $0.15 = $150. Rework: 100 * $0.30 = $30. Total $430. Accepted outputs 900. Cost per accepted output = $430 / 900 = $0.47778, rounded $0.48.
Assumptions: A 70% accepted, B 90% accepted. Review unit costs differ by model because effort varies in this fictional illustration. Rework costs are charged on rejected outputs. Excludes infrastructure, compliance, incident response and switching costs. In a real procurement, add these before making a decision. The comparison does not demonstrate net ROI without measuring value delivered and cash effects.
