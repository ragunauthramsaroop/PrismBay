## Verified news hook and editorial boundary

On 15 October 2026 the Financial Stability Board published a final report setting out **12 sound practices** for responsible AI adoption by financial institutions. The FSB strongly encourages boards and senior management to use the practices in developing AI governance and controls. These are **non-binding sound practices, not a new law, certification, or universally mandatory standard**. FSB groups the practices into organisation-wide governance and AI-lifecycle risk management. Its discussion covers generative and agentic AI.

## Finished spoken script (approx. 125 words)

“AI agents are reaching the boardroom. On October fifteenth, the Financial Stability Board published twelve sound practices for responsible AI adoption in finance. This is guidance, not a new law.

Here is the practical test for an executive team.

First: name the accountable human. Who owns the outcome when an agent acts?

Second: draw the permission boundary. What data and systems can the agent access, and which actions require approval?

Third: define the stop rule. What error, unusual transaction, or security event pauses the workflow?

For example, an agent can prepare a supplier-payment recommendation. It should not silently change banking details or release funds.

The point is not to ban automation. It is to assign authority, limit actions, and preserve evidence.

PrismBay AI explains practical AI business systems for founders and decision-makers.”
