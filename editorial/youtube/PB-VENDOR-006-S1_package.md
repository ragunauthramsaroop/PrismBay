## Editorial promise
A contract-screening checklist focused on the operational questions a buyer should resolve before granting a vendor access to business data. This is educational guidance, not legal advice or a universal legal checklist.

## Narration, ~132 words
"Before you sign an AI vendor contract, stop at the data clause. Ask six questions.
One: What information will the system receive?
Two: Will your inputs or outputs train a model, and under which settings?
Three: Which subcontractors or subprocessors will handle your information?
Four: How long is the information stored, and what happens when you leave?
Five: Who reports a security incident, and how quickly?
Six: If the model produces a costly error, what responsibility does the contract assign?
Now apply the test. Imagine a vendor summarizing confidential supplier bids. A good demo is not proof that the data terms protect your business.
Request written answers, map them to the contract, and have your legal and security owners review the exceptions before access begins.
