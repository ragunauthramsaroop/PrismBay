## Ready-to-use YouTube description, after final QA approval
Your AI agent writes a convincing recommendation. Who gave the agent permission to execute it?

This original PrismBay AI tutorial shows founders, CFOs, operations leaders and procurement teams how to map business actions to four levels of authority: OBSERVE, DRAFT, PROPOSE and EXECUTE. We work through a fictional supplier invoice and purchasing workflow, explain independent approval controls, and test what happens when a document contains hostile instructions or a reviewer rejects an action.

You will learn how to:
• Separate reading, drafting, proposing and executing.
• Inventory sensitive actions, including sending, paying, deleting, exporting and changing bank details.
• Enforce permissions in tools and back-end systems, rather than relying on a prompt.
• Evaluate released capacity, verified cash effects and control exceptions.
• Run five sandbox tests before expanding an agent's authority.
