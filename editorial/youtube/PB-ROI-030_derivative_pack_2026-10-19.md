- Audience: CFOs, finance managers, operations directors, small-business founders.
- Target duration: 43–53 seconds. Format: 1080 × 1920. Hook type: counterintuitive numerical result.
- Title: **AI Saved 67 Hours. The Cash Saving Was Only $90.**

### PB-ROI-030-S3 | The three-gate stop rule
- Experiment hypothesis: a crisp executive decision rule produces qualified subscriptions even with lower raw views.
- Audience: founders, executives, risk and compliance owners.
- Target duration: 43–55 seconds. Hook type: decision framework.
- Title: **Don't Scale an AI Pilot Until These 3 Gates Pass**

- 27–40s: "Third, risk. Confirm data permissions, human approvals and an immediate stop owner."
- 40–50s: "If one gate fails, fix the workflow before scaling. A demo is not an investment case."
- Closing CTA: "PrismBay AI helps leaders build AI systems with measurable outcomes."
- Unique visual grammar: original three-door decision diagram, each door opening only after evidence is shown. No reuse of receipt or ledger scenes.
- On-screen label: "VALUE / QUALITY / RISK".
- Success gate: subscribers per 100 views and comment quality take priority over view count, provided sample size is sufficient.
