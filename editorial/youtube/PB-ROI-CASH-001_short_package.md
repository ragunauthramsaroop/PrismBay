## Core distinction
Time released is an operational capacity measure. Cash saved requires an identifiable reduction in spending or a verified incremental cash contribution. Do not imply that capacity released equals reduced payroll. All numerical examples are hypothetical, not PrismBay or client outcomes.

## Short 2: Four Numbers Before You Renew an AI Tool
ID: PB-ROI-CASH-001-S2 | 48–58 seconds | Style: four-lane live pilot scorecard and one red control-failure animation, visibly distinct from S1.
Narration:
"Before renewing an AI tool, ask for four numbers. One: capacity released, measured in hours. Two: verified cash effect, after subscription, oversight and setup costs. Three: quality, such as correction rates and turnaround time. Four: control exceptions, including actions attempted without approval. Here's the trap: a pilot saves thirty hours, but errors double and the system sends a supplier change without authorization. The time saving doesn't erase the control failure. Set a pass rule before the pilot starts. If quality or controls fail, pause the rollout, even if the time chart looks impressive. PrismBay AI helps leaders build AI systems they can measure and govern."
