## S1 | AI Saved 67 Hours. Cash Saved? Maybe $0.
Estimated 47–53 seconds, pace ~135 words/minute. Original numerical example, not a real company claim.

[0–2s] "Your AI saved sixty-seven hours. Your CFO says cash saved: zero."
[2–8s] "Both numbers can be correct. Here's the difference."
[8–18s] "A team handles one thousand supplier requests. Twelve minutes each is two hundred hours."
[18–28s] "With AI assistance, each request takes eight minutes. That's about one hundred thirty-three hours. Sixty-seven hours released."
[28–38s] "But released time is capacity, not a bank deposit. If payroll and other spending stay unchanged, realized cash savings can still be zero."
[38–47s] "Track two separate ledgers: hours released and expenses actually eliminated. Never add overlapping benefits."
[47–53s] "PrismBay AI explains practical AI systems with numbers, not hype."

Visual beats: 1,000 × 12m = 200h → 1,000 × 8m = 133h → 67h capacity. Then split ledger: TIME RELEASED 67h / VERIFIED CASH $0 in zero-cash scenario.
On-screen footnote: Hypothetical illustration. $0 assumes no expense avoided. Never present the zero-cash scenario as the original long-form expected case.

## S2 | Before Scaling an AI Agent, Pass These 3 Gates
Estimated 45–52 seconds, pace ~135 words/minute.

[0–3s] "A faster AI workflow is not ready to scale until three gates pass."
[3–16s] "Gate one: financial value. Separate staff capacity from expenses truly avoided. Count software, integration, and review costs."
[16–29s] "Gate two: quality. Sample outputs and compare error rates against your pre-pilot baseline. Do not hide difficult cases."
[29–42s] "Gate three: control. Keep human approvals, permission limits, audit logs, and a stop switch for serious failures."
[42–52s] "If one gate fails, fix the pilot before expansion. Follow PrismBay AI for measurable business automation."

Visual beats: FINANCIAL → QUALITY → RISK, each checkmark appearing after a concrete test. Closing: EXPAND / MODIFY / STOP.
