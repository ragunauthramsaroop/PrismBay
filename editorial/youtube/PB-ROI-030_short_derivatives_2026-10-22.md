## Experiment S3: The stop rule
Audience: procurement, risk, compliance and AI implementation leaders.
Hypothesis: a governance checklist with a decisive stop trigger attracts high-intent business viewers.
Target length: 44-54 seconds. Hook: risk question. Format: animated approval workflow map, with branches and risk gates.
Title options:
1. The AI Pilot Stop Rule Every Company Needs
2. AI Saved Time. Why Should You Still Shut It Down?
3. Three Gates Before You Scale an AI Agent
First-frame text: "FASTER ≠ SAFE TO SCALE"
Narration:
"Your AI pilot processes requests thirty percent faster. Should you scale it? Not until it passes three gates. First, economics: measure actual expenses avoided and full operating cost, not only minutes saved. Second, quality: compare errors, corrections, and unresolved exceptions with your pre-pilot baseline. Third, control: keep approvals with accountable people, log recommendations, and define a stop trigger for unauthorized actions or data exposure. If the financial case is weak, modify the workflow. If quality worsens, pause expansion. If a critical control fails, stop the system and investigate. A faster process is not a successful process unless it is financially defensible and safe."
