## Short 3: The Three Gates Before You Scale an AI Pilot
Length target: 40–50 seconds. Audience: operations and compliance leaders. Hook: decision test.
Narration: "A positive ROI spreadsheet is not enough to scale an AI pilot. Run three gates. First, did verified financial results or capacity outcomes meet the agreed target? Second, did accuracy and service quality hold up? Third, did the system respect data boundaries and human approval requirements? In a supplier workflow, AI can draft and classify requests. People retain approval of payments and supplier banking changes. If a serious control fails, pause and investigate. Expand only when value, quality and risk controls all pass. Subscribe to PrismBay AI for practical, accountable AI business systems."
