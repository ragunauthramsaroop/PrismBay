## Short 1 | PB-ROI-030-S1 | Capacity is not cash
Audience: CFOs, operations directors, founders.
Hook type: surprising arithmetic contradiction.
Hypothesis: showing a large capacity gain beside a tiny verified net saving improves completion and subscriber conversion over generic AI-ROI claims.
Target: 42–52 seconds. Format: animated original split-ledger and moving number bars, no stock montage.
Working title: "AI Saved 67 Hours. The Cash Benefit Was Only $90."
Alternative hook for future sequential test, not duplicate upload: "Your AI dashboard says $2,333 saved. Finance says $90."
Voice script (~105 words):
"Your AI pilot saved sixty-seven hours. So why did the finance team approve only ninety dollars in monthly net benefit? Here's the difference. A hypothetical team handles one thousand requests a month. AI assistance cuts average handling time from twelve minutes to eight. That's sixty-six point seven hours released. At thirty-five dollars an hour, the capacity value is about twenty-three hundred dollars. But no payroll cost automatically disappears. If thirty overtime hours are truly eliminated, that's one thousand fifty dollars in cash avoided. Subtract nine hundred sixty dollars in recurring AI costs. Net cash benefit: ninety dollars. Time released matters. Cash saved needs proof. PrismBay AI explains practical AI business systems."
On-screen storyboard: 0–2s two figures collide "67 HOURS / $90"; 2–9s original 1,000-request stack; 9–18s timeline 12 min → 8 min; 18–26s 66.7h capacity counter; 26–36s crossed-out "66.7h = cash"; 36–45s actual overtime $1,050 less cost $960 = $90; 45–52s "CAPACITY ≠ CASH" and channel promise.
Accuracy gate: 1,000 × (12−8) / 60 = 66.6667 hours; 30 × $35 = $1,050; $1,050 − $960 = $90.
Thumbnail/first frame: no more than two dominant numbers. CTA: "Follow for AI decisions grounded in real numbers."

## Short 2 | PB-ROI-030-S2 | Payback trap
Audience: procurement heads, CFOs, founders.
Hook type: decision question with explicit payback.
Hypothesis: a specific payback calculation attracts high-intent viewers seeking vendor-selection criteria.
Target: 42–55 seconds. Format: animated break-even line chart with one point sliding across a 17-month axis, distinct from S1 ledger visuals.
Working title: "Would You Buy an AI Tool With a 17-Month Payback?"
Voice script (~106 words):
"Would you approve an AI tool that takes nearly seventeen months to recover its setup cost? Here's a simple hypothetical. The pilot removes one thousand fifty dollars of verified overtime each month. Software, integration, and quality review cost nine hundred sixty dollars monthly. That leaves only ninety dollars in monthly net cash benefit. If setup costs fifteen hundred dollars, divide fifteen hundred by ninety. The simple payback is sixteen point seven months. Now test the downside: if only fifteen overtime hours disappear, the pilot loses four hundred thirty-five dollars a month. The decision isn't whether AI feels productive. It's whether the economics survive scrutiny. Follow PrismBay AI for practical AI business systems."

Thumbnail/first frame: a single horizontal payback ruler with "17 MONTHS".

## Short 3 | PB-ROI-030-S3 | Three gates to scale
Audience: COOs, IT leaders, risk and compliance executives.
Hook type: actionable governance rule.
Hypothesis: a concrete three-gate decision system creates higher saves and shares than a numerical ROI hook.
Target: 38–48 seconds. Format: custom three-door decision diagram with risk icons, no reused ledger/graph template.
Working title: "Before Scaling Any AI Agent, Pass These 3 Gates."
Voice script (~99 words):
"Before you scale an AI agent, make it pass three gates. First, the value gate. Separate staff time released from expenses truly avoided, and include review, integration, and monitoring costs. Second, the quality gate. Compare error rates and rework against the baseline, including difficult exceptions. Third, the control gate. Restrict permissions, preserve human approval for sensitive actions, and record what the agent did. If any gate fails, don't scale. Fix the workflow or stop the pilot. A thirty-day scorecard should tell leaders what to expand, what to redesign, and what to retire. PrismBay AI covers practical AI business systems."
Storyboard: 0–2s "3 GATES"; 2–13s original cash/clock icon; 13–25s error-distribution graphic; 25–37s permission boundary and human signoff; 37–46s 3-gate decision flow.
Source grounding: NIST AI RMF Govern/Map/Measure/Manage, not a certification or guarantee. https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework
Proposed time slot: 16:00 America/Guyana on a separate day.
Success threshold: compare subscriber conversion and saves/shares against the prior two Shorts and channel baseline. Never judge from raw views alone.
