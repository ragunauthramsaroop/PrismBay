Hypothesis: downside-scenario math draws higher completion than broad AI tips.
Hook type: risk reversal; audience: executives approving budgets.
Title: "One Assumption Turned This AI Pilot Negative"
Script:
"An AI pilot looks profitable until one assumption changes. Here's a hypothetical case. Recurring AI costs are nine hundred sixty dollars per month. If the project truly eliminates thirty overtime hours at thirty-five dollars an hour, it avoids one thousand fifty dollars and nets ninety. But if only fifteen overtime hours disappear, avoided cost falls to five hundred twenty-five dollars. Net result: a loss of four hundred thirty-five dollars per month. Before approving an AI rollout, test expected, downside and upside cases. A credible ROI model survives questions, not applause."
Visual: an original interactive-style slider 30h -> 15h; animated ledger transitions +$90 -> -$435.
