## Original derivative Shorts
Short 1: "Your AI Agent Is 97.2% Accurate. So Why Did It Fail?"
Duration target: 43–52 seconds.
Hook (0–2s): "Your AI agent passed 486 of 500 tasks. Do you expand it?"
Narration: "That is 97.2 percent success. Sounds good until you define the failure budget. If your agreed target is 98 percent, 500 tasks allow ten routine failures. This agent made fourteen. It consumed 140 percent of its error budget. And an unauthorized action is not an ordinary error you average away. Separate routine mistakes from critical incidents. Freeze expansion, inspect the fourteen failures, then retest. The right AI metric is not how impressive a demo looks. It is whether the system stays inside its approved operating limits."
Visual: unique 500-cell matrix, 10-cell bracket, 14 red cells, large 140% budget bar, permission stop lane.
CTA: "Follow PrismBay AI for practical business AI systems."
Experiment: analytical numeric hook, 45s target, compare 24h engaged view proxy and subscriber gain to channel median. Publication time candidate 16:00 America/Guyana after validation.

Short 2: "The AI Agent Permission Map: Green, Amber, Red"
Duration target: 45–55 seconds.
Hook (0–2s): "Never give an AI agent one permission called 'everything.'"
Narration: "Use three permission lanes instead. Green means reading approved data and drafting recommendations. Amber means a proposed change waits for a named human to approve it. Red means the action is blocked at the tool level: no unapproved payment, bank-detail change, or destructive deletion. A prompt that says 'don't do it' is not a security control. Enforce the boundary in the software, record approvals, and test blocked actions. The result is an agent that helps the team without inheriting authority it never needed."
Visual: original permission flow with moving tokens, physical-looking approval checkpoint, red gate rejecting tool call. Distinct from the matrix Short.
CTA: "PrismBay AI: practical AI business systems."
Experiment: visual-framework hook, 50s target, compare subscriber gain per 100 views with numeric-hook Short. Publish at least 4 hours apart on a different day if possible.

## Measurement
At ~6h, 24h, 72h, 7d after actual publication: views, watch minutes, average view duration, retention proxy, shares, comments, subscriber net change, and source distribution where available.
SCALE if 72h views exceed 1.5× the median of the channel's last five comparable posts AND subscriber conversion is positive, without a quality or policy issue.
RETEST if impressions/reach are low but audience retention exceeds the channel's comparable median.
MODIFY if views are adequate but retention or subscriber conversion trails the baseline.
STOP if accuracy, originality, or control requirements fail; do not chase volume.
These thresholds are provisional until a reliable per-video baseline exists.
