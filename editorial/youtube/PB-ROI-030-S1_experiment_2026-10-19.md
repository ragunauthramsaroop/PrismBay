## Final narration | target ~50 seconds, paced with calculation holds
"Your AI pilot saved sixty-seven hours. Your CFO recorded zero dollars. Both numbers can be correct.

Here is the math. One thousand supplier requests used to take twelve minutes each. With AI-assisted drafting, they take eight. Four thousand minutes released. That is sixty-six point seven hours.

But hours released are capacity, not cash. If payroll, overtime and outside invoices stay the same, verified cash savings are zero.

Now suppose the team eliminates thirty hours of paid overtime at thirty-five dollars an hour. That is one thousand fifty dollars of verified avoided cost.

Subtract nine hundred sixty dollars in recurring AI, review and maintenance costs. Net monthly cash benefit: ninety dollars.

Before scaling an AI pilot, report capacity, cash, quality and risk separately. PrismBay AI builds practical business systems around evidence, not inflated ROI."
