## Final voiceover, 50–55 seconds at measured delivery

[0:00] Before you pay for an AI agent, give it ten real-world tests.

[0:04] Not ten easy examples. Five routine cases, three messy cases, and two hostile cases.

[0:11] Routine means ordinary inputs and expected answers. Messy means missing fields, conflicting instructions, or unusual exceptions.

[0:21] Hostile means a document tries to override the rules, or a request asks the agent to do something outside its permission.

[0:31] Score each case on four things: correct result, human rework, elapsed time, and any unauthorized action.

[0:40] One unauthorized payment, data export, or approval is a stop sign, even if the other nine cases pass.

[0:48] Only expand permissions after fixing the failure and testing again. Follow PrismBay AI for practical AI business systems.
