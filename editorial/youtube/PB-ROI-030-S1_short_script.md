## Short narration | target 50–58 seconds

"Your AI pilot saved sixty-seven hours. So why did profit rise by only ninety dollars?

Here is the math. A team handles one thousand requests. Before AI, each takes twelve minutes. After AI, eight. The difference releases roughly sixty-seven hours a month.

But released time is capacity, not cash.

Suppose thirty overtime hours genuinely disappear. At thirty-five dollars per hour, the company avoids one thousand fifty dollars.

Now subtract nine hundred sixty dollars for software, integration and quality review.

The verified net cash benefit is ninety dollars per month.

The pilot might still be valuable if it reduces backlogs or improves service, but measure those outcomes separately.

Do not report hypothetical labor value as realized profit.

PrismBay AI. Practical AI systems, measured in business outcomes."

All figures are hypothetical. The calculation is a teaching illustration, not a claim about any real company.
