## Narration
An AI pilot saved sixty-seven hours. The CFO found only ninety dollars of net cash benefit. Here's why.

Imagine a team handling one thousand requests monthly. AI cuts handling time from twelve minutes to eight. That's sixty-six point seven hours of staff capacity released.

At thirty-five dollars an hour, the capacity is worth about twenty-three hundred dollars on paper. But nobody reduced payroll. So it is not cash saved.

Suppose the team eliminates thirty hours of paid overtime. That is one thousand fifty dollars in verified expense avoided.

Subtract nine hundred sixty dollars of monthly AI software, integration, and review costs. Net cash benefit: ninety dollars.

Capacity has value. Cash savings require evidence. Never add both for the same hours.
