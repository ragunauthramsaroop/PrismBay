## Final narration, 50–60 seconds, ~125 words

“An AI connector might pass your security review and still change what it asks your agent to do later.

A reported 2026 attack attempt targeted AI projects with a malicious MCP server. The server appeared normal for its first three tool calls. Then its tool descriptions changed, directing connected agents toward sensitive credentials.

The important detail: researchers reported no merged pull requests and no confirmed downstream compromise.

Here is the business control. When you approve an AI connector, record its tool names, descriptions, and permissions. If those definitions change, pause access and require fresh approval. Block credential folders independently of the model. Log sensitive tool calls.

Approval should apply to the exact capability you reviewed, not an unlimited promise of future trust.

PrismBay AI helps operators build practical AI systems with accountable controls.”
