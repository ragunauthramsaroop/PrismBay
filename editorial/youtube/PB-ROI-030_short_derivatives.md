## PB-ROI-030-S3 | “Three Gates Before You Scale an AI Agent”
Experiment: risk-led decision framework. Target procurement and operations leads. 45–55 seconds, 9:16. First frame “DON'T SCALE YET”.

“Your AI pilot processes requests faster. Should you roll it out company-wide? Only after three gates. First, the financial gate. Have you documented cash savings, or a measurable capacity benefit, after the full operating cost? Second, the quality gate. Did error rates, rework and customer outcomes hold steady or improve? Third, the risk gate. Are confidential data, approvals and exceptional cases under human control? In a payment workflow, the agent can draft and route, but it must not approve a transfer without authorized human review. If all three gates pass, consider a controlled expansion. If one is weak, modify the pilot. If a critical control fails, stop. PrismBay AI turns AI ideas into accountable business systems.”
