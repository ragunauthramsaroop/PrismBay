## Narration (approximately 48 seconds)
"Your AI pilot saved forty hours. Did you save forty hours of wages? Not necessarily. Here is the math. Suppose eight hundred requests each take three fewer minutes. Eight hundred times three divided by sixty equals forty hours of capacity. But if payroll stays unchanged, the cash saving from payroll is zero. To prove financial return, document an expense that went away, such as verified overtime or contractor costs. Then subtract software, review, integration and maintenance expenses. Keep four separate measures: time released, cash avoided, service quality and control failures. Released time is useful. Verified financial return is different.
