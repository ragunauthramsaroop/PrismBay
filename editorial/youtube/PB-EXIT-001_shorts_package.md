## Hypothesis and audience
Founder, COO, CFO, procurement head or IT leader considering a paid AI workflow tool. A concrete exit-risk scenario with a usable four-part checklist will attract higher-quality subscribers than generic model news. Test the thesis without claiming vendor-specific defects.

## Primary title
Before You Sign an AI Vendor Contract, Run This Exit Test

Alternative title 1: Your AI Pilot Works. What Happens When You Leave?
Alternative title 2: The AI Procurement Question Nobody Wants to Answer

## Original 49–55-second voiceover
[0:00–0:03] Your AI pilot works. Now ask the vendor one uncomfortable question: what happens when we leave?

[0:03–0:10] A demo proves a workflow works today. It does not prove you can recover your data, prompts, audit trail and integrations tomorrow.

[0:10–0:15] Before signing, run a four-part exit test.

[0:15–0:24] One: export. Request a sample export in a documented, usable format, not screenshots.

[0:24–0:33] Two: portability. Identify what you must rebuild: prompts, rules, connectors and approval steps.

[0:33–0:42] Three: deletion. Get the retention, backup and deletion process in writing. Check contractual obligations and legal exceptions.

[0:42–0:49] Four: continuity. Estimate the downtime and staff effort to move the workflow.

[0:49–0:55] If you cannot price the exit, you do not know the full cost. PrismBay AI: practical AI systems for business leaders.
