## Master narration

"Your AI support bot reports eighty percent deflection. Finance asks: how much cash did we save?

Here's the test. Suppose the bot costs nine hundred dollars a month, plus three hundred for reviews and monitoring. Total: twelve hundred.

Your support contractor charges four dollars per resolved ticket, but only when a ticket is handled.

Break-even is twelve hundred divided by four: three hundred genuinely avoided contractor tickets.

Not three hundred chatbot conversations. Not three hundred auto-closed cases. Resolved tickets with no repeat contact, no escalation, and a lower contractor invoice.

At two hundred fifty avoided tickets, you lose two hundred dollars. At four hundred, you net four hundred.

If the contract is fixed and the bill never falls, record capacity released, not cash saved.

That is the difference between a dashboard success and a finance-approved business case.

PrismBay AI helps operators build practical AI business systems."

All financial figures are hypothetical illustrations. A variable per-ticket contract is an explicit assumption, not a claim about typical contracts. The arithmetic assumes no other variable costs, benefits, or offsets. Do not describe ticket avoidance as independently verified in this example.
