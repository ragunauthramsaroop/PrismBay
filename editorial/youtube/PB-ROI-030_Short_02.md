## Narration
Your AI pilot costs four hundred twenty dollars a month. Or does it?

That is only the software line. Let's price the whole workflow.

Suppose the subscription and model usage cost four hundred twenty dollars monthly. Add three hundred for integration and maintenance. Add two hundred forty for quality checks and human review.

The recurring cost is nine hundred sixty dollars. More than double the headline software price.

Now suppose you verify one thousand fifty dollars of avoided overtime. Net cash benefit: ninety dollars a month.

The wrong calculation says six hundred thirty dollars saved. The complete calculation says ninety.

Before approving an AI pilot, ask for a full cost ledger, including monitoring, exceptions, and corrections.
