## Derivative Short S1 | “Hours Are Not Cash” | 45–55 seconds
Hypothesis: A sharp accounting contradiction will earn high completion among finance and operations viewers.
Hook (first frame and first words): “Your AI saved 67 hours. Your cash savings might still be zero.”
Narration (~108 words):
“Your AI saved sixty-seven hours this month. Great. Now answer one question: Which expense disappeared? If the team still receives the same salaries, and no overtime or contractor invoice was reduced, your verified cash savings might be zero. You gained capacity, which has value, but capacity and cash belong in different columns. Here's a hypothetical example. One thousand requests, four minutes faster each, releases sixty-six point seven hours. Do not multiply those hours by salary and call the result profit. Track backlog cleared and service speed separately. Track cash only when finance verifies an avoided cost. PrismBay AI turns AI promises into measurable business decisions.”

## Derivative Short S3 | “The Risk Gate” | 45–55 seconds
Hypothesis: A clear permission-boundary demonstration converts governance-focused executives into subscribers.
Hook: “An AI pilot with positive ROI can still fail approval.”
Narration (~107 words):
“Suppose your AI pilot reduces operating costs. Before you scale it, ask three different questions. First: Did finance verify the benefit, including every recurring cost? Second: Did service quality improve, or did errors and rework erase the gain? Third: Could the system access confidential records, change supplier banking details, or approve payments without a human? If the answer to the third question is yes, stop the rollout and fix the permission boundary. A good AI scorecard has three gates: financial value, quality and risk. A green ROI number cannot override a failed control. PrismBay AI helps leaders build systems worth trusting.”
Visual grammar: original process flow diagram, AI draft arrow ending at a human approval checkpoint, blocked banking-change branch in red. No stock robot footage.
CTA: “Subscribe for practical AI governance and workflow design.”
Shorts packaging: Title “Positive AI ROI? This One Control Can Stop the Rollout.” First-frame caption “ROI PASS ≠ APPROVAL.”
