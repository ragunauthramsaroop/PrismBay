Privacy Policy

How customer information is handled when you browse PrismBay Clean or place an order.

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Business operator

PrismBay Clean is a customer-facing retail brand operated by STUDYSMARTZ LLC.

Information you provide

Information submitted for an order, delivery or support request may include your name, email address, phone number, shipping address and order details.

Payments

Product buttons route to Stripe-hosted checkout. Payment card details are entered on Stripe's checkout service and are not collected through the PrismBay Clean storefront page.

Fulfillment and delivery

Order information required to process and deliver a purchase may be shared with the relevant fulfillment providers and carriers for that order.

Support

Information sent to customer support is used to locate orders, respond to requests and resolve order issues. Do not send full card numbers or security codes by email.

Third-party services

Stripe and any fulfillment or carrier services involved in an order process information under their own privacy practices and legal obligations.

Privacy contact

For privacy questions related to PrismBay Clean, email support@prismbayai.com.
