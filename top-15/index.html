How competitive pricing works

Three checks before we sell.

We want repeat customers, not one-time orders we cannot fulfill. Every physical item must pass these checks before a payment link appears.

01

Confirm the exact product

Verify supplier identity, model, quality evidence, permissions to use product media, and current stock.

02

Check your delivered cost

Review destination ZIP freight, duties where applicable, processing fees, refunds and fulfillment cost against a comparable market offer.

03

Approve checkout

Enable payment only when the supplier route and order handling are approved. Until then, your request is informational.
