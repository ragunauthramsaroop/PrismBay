Authorize eBay in CJ

  1. Open CJdropshipping and sign in.
  2. Go to My CJ → Authorization → eBay.
  3. Click Add Store.
  4. Enter the exact eBay User ID as the store name.
  5. eBay opens its permission screen. Review it and click I agree.
  6. GitHub will detect the authorized eBay shop on the next control-plane run.
